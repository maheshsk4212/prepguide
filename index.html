10 things to remember

  1. Design system: shared product setup, not a Figma library.
  2. Tokens: raw value → meaning → component → product.
  3. Governance: set the standards centrally, not every decision.
  4. Contribution: let product teams add to it without breaking the structure.
  5. Adoption: measure results, not how many components exist.
  6. Design debt: fix the repeated mess that costs the most later.
  7. Engineering: design and engineering build the system together.
  8. Accessibility: build it in at token, component and pattern level.
  9. AI: speeds up repetitive work. People still make design decisions.
  10. Leadership: turn what one person knows into docs and tools, so teams can work on their own.
