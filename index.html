Only you know these

Fill these in before any call

These are the gaps that keep coming back. Until they’re filled, use the safe line in each answer.

  1. 5 → 2 days handoff: what did you count, and how did you know it was 5 before?
  2. 98% WCAG: which tool or method? What was the missing 2%?
  3. 30% onboarding: onboarding of whom? What took less time?
  4. ClinChoice 20% / 40%: who measured it, and over what period?
  5. 200+ components: roughly how many base parts vs bigger patterns?
  6. Arabic / RTL: did any of your work include it? What exactly?
  7. Crevato: active now? Paid clients? Will you keep it if hired?
  8. Location and visa: recruiter forms say “Bengaluru / India”, but you’re in Dubai. Pick one true answer and use it everywhere. Notice period in your emails: 30 days (can be 15).
  9. Salary: emails show AED 20K/month (Virtusa) and ₹35 LPA (India). Confirm these, and set your lowest AED figure.
  10. A real stakeholder disagreement: who, what, how it ended.
  11. A real mistake that cost time or rework.
  12. A real tight deadline: scope, time, what you cut.

Live design task and portfolio walk-through

Live task in 5 steps

1 Clarify: user, task, goal, limits→2 Pick the one problem that matters most→3 Flow: entry → decide → act → confirm → result→4 Edge cases: error, empty, loading, rights, offline, recovery→5 “I’d test the riskiest guess first”

Talk while you draw. Silence reads as stuck. Say what you are choosing and why, and what you are leaving out.
